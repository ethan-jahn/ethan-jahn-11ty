# High Performance Computing

I'm part of the HPC team at the University of Arizona. We support researchers across many disciplines who utilize the supercomputing resources that are available through UITS Research Technologies. I work closely with faculty, students, and researchers on a daily basis. 

There are always a number of ongoing projects. Two that I am exited about are our [new documentation website](https://hpcdocs.hpc.arizona.edu/) and the [HPC workshop series](https://hpcdocs.hpc.arizona.edu/events/calendar/) that I have been leading for a few years now. 

Given my background in astrophysics, I tend to work particularly closely with researchers at [Steward Observatory](https://astro.arizona.edu/). 

One thing I like about this role is that it's very dynamic, but that also means it's hard to describe. Reach out to me if you're curious!
