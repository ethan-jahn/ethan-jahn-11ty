# Hello

I like making stuff with computers and helping other people make stuff with computers. Sometimes also other tools like cameras and musical instruments. The things I like to make range from scientific simulations of galaxy formations to code that generates silly shapes, technical documentation, and live audio-reactive visuals. 

---

Here is a bio I used for an event recently:

> Ethan Jahn is a computational astrophysicist and consultant for the High Performance Computing systems at the University of Arizona, where he supports researchers and their workflows across academic disciplines. His background as an Adjunct Professor of Physics informs his current role leading HPC workshops, which range from foundational topics like Intro to HPC, Linux, and data management to specialized areas such as parallel computing and machine learning. His work as a digital artist has led to cross-disciplinary collaborations, and he continues to explore the connections between computing technology and research across fields, working to translate that knowledge for researchers at all levels.

---

You might (or might not) be wondering about the domain -- "digital-rhizome.net" is my personal domain for self-hosted services, including:

- this website
- a nextcloud file server
- a joplin server instance which syncs all my notes
- my personal music library from ripped CDs and bandcamp purchases
- a remote ssh gateway
- a netdata dashboard

This project is a materialization of my preference for DIY and rejection of the big-tech sphere where multi-billion dollar companies harvest our user data and barely pay the artists who make their platform possible. I'm working on a self-hosting guide to show you how to do this as well!

Long live small tech! 

Shrink the internet!
