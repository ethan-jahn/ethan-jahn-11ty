I use a program called TouchDesigner to render real-time projection graphics for live events. This can include a camcorder pointed at the projection region whose signal is blended with the generated graphics to produce feedback effects. 

This work has been presented thrice:

1. (de)composition live interactive show, [Golden Saguaro](https://goldensaguaro.org/), February 2026
2. Echoes of the Monoliths, Trona Pinnacles, April 2026
3. Alfred and the Breeders house show, May 2026

My system is ready to go, and easily customized. If you are interested in having this presented at your event, please contact me on [instagram](https://www.instagram.com/xthnj.wav/).
